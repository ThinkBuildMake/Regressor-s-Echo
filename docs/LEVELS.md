# Level Plan

Rooms connect Celeste-style (walk through a gap, the camera slides over). Each room is a puzzle you can see whole. Decisions behind this live in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).

## Rules for World 1
- **1 soul.** A new death moves your only body.
- **No choosing to die.** Every death comes from the forest: spikes, thorns, the walker. Die-anywhere arrives in World 3 when the amulet awakens.
- **Shrines** (mid-room checkpoints) let a 1-soul puzzle chain: place the soul, cross, touch the shrine, place it again.
- No slide yet (it arrives in World 2).

## World 1: Forest Edge (7 rooms, about 4–5 minutes)

| # | Room | What it teaches | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The clearing | Moving and jumping. He finds the amulet and puts it on | Cocky grumbling, then the curious eyebrow raise and a greedy grin at the amulet |
| 2 | The ghost | The guide appears | The opening conversation (below) |
| 3 | First death | A spike pit too wide to jump. Jump in: you die, come back, and your soul floats over the spikes as a stepping stone | First death: pained face, "That… hurt." |
| 4 | The thorn wall | Height: die against thorns at the top of a jump to make a step | The guide teaches timing |
| 5 | The patrol | A switch holds a door open; a walker patrols over it. Let it kill you on the switch, so your soul holds it down | Guide: "I want you to get killed *efficiently*." |
| 6 | The chain | Two placements with a shrine between: bridge a pit, reach the shrine, die again to make a step up | "How many times have you done this?" "Today? Or ever?" …"Fine." |
| 7 | The moth | Boss 1: a wing-dust attack you only survive behind your soul; stomp it 3 times; dying heals it | Guide, quietly: "Careful. It remembers too." |

After the moth: the amulet grows stronger and you earn a **2nd soul**, leading into World 2.

## Opening conversation (room 2)

The dialogue lives in [dialogue.js](../dialogue.js). The guide is playful, mischievous and witty; the one serious line is marked.

> **Guide** *(mischievous)*: Well, well. Which death are you currently on?
> **Hero** *(startled)*: Death? I'm on?
> **Guide** *(amused)*: Oh, a first-timer! This is my favorite part.
> **Guide** *(smug)*: Introductions, then. I'm you. Give or take a few thousand deaths.
> **Hero** *(annoyed)*: You're me? I'm way better looking.
> **Guide** *(teasing)*: Same face. Same hair. Same bad decisions.
> **Guide** *(wry)*: Every death sends you back. Your body forgets. You don't.
> **Hero** *(excited)*: So this amulet makes me immortal? What's it worth?
> **Guide** *(amused)*: Nope! Not telling.
> **Guide** *(warning, the armor slips)*: …It won't let you stay dead. One day you'll wish it would.
> **Guide** *(mischievous)*: Anyway! Treasure's that way. Try not to die. …Actually, do. It's the only way through.

First death: **Hero** *(shouting)*: AGH! THAT HURT! · **Guide** *(wry)*: It's not like you actually died, right? · **Hero** *(annoyed)*: I literally just died.
