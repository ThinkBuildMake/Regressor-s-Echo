# 1. Damage beyond stomping

**Best: turn a blocked attack into a counterattack.** Players see their death placement become the weapon, using collisions you already need.

Treat these as alternatives: build **one damage method per boss**, with three successful counters winning. Avoid six competing vulnerability rules.

### Develop the falling-stone idea — Stag, W3

An antler-shaped stone falls onto a soul above the charge lane. The stag charges beneath it and hits a buttress; during its 2s daze, recall the supporting soul to drop the stone onto its back.

- Make the support **newest**; two older souls handle protection.
- Show cracks and falling grit 0.8s before the stone drops.
- A missed stone breaks on the floor; another arrives next cycle.
- The player must build the support during setup. Recalling cannot create its replacement without another death.
- Consequently, use **one final stone hit**, or provide three stones supported by three souls and remove competing soul requirements.

### Moth — ranked alternatives, W1

All use one soul placed through a warned hazard; none require recall.

1. **Dust return:** a slow yellow pod hits a soul, reverses horizontally, and bursts against the moth during its recovery.
2. **Dive collision:** bait its horizontal dive into your soul. Impact damages it; the soul survives.
3. **Pinned wing:** a descending moth lands on the soul, catching its wing briefly; touch the exposed glowing abdomen to damage it.
4. **Silk tension:** a volley leaves a thread attached to the soul. The moth’s next crossing snaps the thread across its body.
5. **Spore trap:** dust accumulates on the soul; three deposits make a puff that damages the moth when it swoops through.

### Tree — ranked alternatives, W2

1. **Pod batting:** a heavy seed bounces upward off a soul into the mask. Position one soul as the bat and the other as shelter.
2. **Decoy overreach:** bait the locked slam onto the outer soul; the arm strikes it and recoils into the mask.
3. **Branch fulcrum:** a low soul catches the swinging branch; its far end pivots upward into the face. Animate the rotation; use box hit zones.
4. **Root fuse:** a seed caught on a soul sprouts a glowing root toward the trunk. Preserve it through one attack; it snaps back and damages the tree.
5. **Counterweight:** a soul holds a stone basket’s plate down, raising a hammer. A third death removes that oldest soul and releases it. **Setup-only or finishing blow:** that death heals the boss first.

### Stag — ranked alternatives, W3

1. **Recall-drop stone:** the developed idea above.
2. **Antler snare:** two souls span a narrow charge gate; the stag catches between them. Recall the newest to twist it into a buttress.
3. **Beam return:** a marked crystal rests on a soul and reflects the beam toward the stag. Recall lowers it into firing height.
4. **Suspended ram:** recall a soul holding a horizontal spring-loaded log; the released log strikes the dazed stag.
5. **Exposed heart:** a soul blocks the stag’s returning spectral orb. While the orb remains trapped, its heart opens for a normal touch attack.

# 2. Moth patterns

**Best: Lantern Dive.** It makes the soul both cover and a deliberately placed collision target.

| Rank / attack | Tell and timing | Soul answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Lantern Dive** | Wings fold, yellow silhouette marks a horizontal lane for 0.8s; dive 0.5s; recover 1.2s. | Hide behind the soul; collision triggers damage. |
| 2. **Dust Loom** | Wings shake 0.6s; three aimed pods, 0.35s apart. Aim locks separately. | Move around your soul so each pod hits it. |
| 3. **Pollen Curtain** | Ceiling dots brighten 1s; falling dust fills three broad strips sequentially. | Shelter beneath the soul; its footprint cuts a safe opening. |
| 4. **Ground Skimmer** | Moth descends visibly for 0.8s; sweeps the bottom tile. | Stand on the soul, then reposition during recovery. |

**Pattern:** Loom → Dive → Curtain → reposition pause. Never select attacks randomly without recovery.

**Phase change after first damage:** wings tear; add Skimmer between loops. After second, Loom fires four pods. Keep tells unchanged; shorten recoveries modestly. Give the arena a clearly warned thorn patch for choosing soul height.

# 3. Tree and stag patterns

**Best: retain the tree’s locked decoy slam.** It already turns two souls into distinct tools and rewards reading intent.

### Tree

1. **Borrowed Fist:** blue arm glow, tether to the nearest soul, lock immediately; slam after 2.05s. Bait with the decoy, retreat onto the step. Use recoil damage or open the mask.
2. **Seed Mortar:** three yellow landing circles appear 0.7s before impact. Lead them away, or intercept the final heavy seed with the batting soul.
3. **Root Rake:** roots ripple outward 0.8s before sweeping the floor. Souls provide standing refuge; branches pass through them.
4. **False Spring:** the buried soul visibly pushes upward during its final second. Its return announces the next cycle.

Loop Rake → Mortar → Slam. Speed up movement between attacks, preserving warning windows. Burial must end before the next required decoy slam.

### Stag

1. **Buttress Charge:** hoof scrape and yellow lane for 0.9s; charge, collision, 2s daze. This is the stone-drop window.
2. **Searching Antlers:** blue aiming line tracks for 1.4s, locks for 0.4s, then fires. Lead it away from structural souls.
3. **Procession:** three yellow floor pulses march toward the platform, each marked 0.6s ahead. Climb your soul staircase.
4. **Last Bell:** the mandatory shelter attack below.

Loop Charge → Beam → Procession → Charge. Trigger Last Bell at a fixed loop boundary. Recall should change a prepared structure during a safe window; it cannot repair lost souls.

# 4. The undodgeable attack

**Best: a reusable shelter check.** It tests preparation without forcing a death after every success.

The current three-thick wave is readable, but consumes two souls. Recall cannot replace them, and dying heals the stag. Requiring another wave therefore undermines progress.

Ranked alternatives:

1. **Three seals:** three marked sockets require souls simultaneously. A violet pulse hits each; occupied sockets cancel the arena blast. Souls survive. Three descending rings telegraph a 3s countdown.
2. **Roof and shutter:** ceiling rain requires an overhead soul; a simultaneous side beam requires a side soul. Two direction arrows fill over 2.5s; a bright rectangle previews the sheltered pocket.
3. **Depth shield:** keep the three-thick wall, but each soul absorbs one layer without breaking. Three violet bands visibly peel off as they cross it.

“Undodgeable” should mean **no movement-only solution**, with the arrangement and countdown visible.

# 5. Rooms

**Best: Saw Exchange.** One death changes two connected obstacles.

1. **Saw Exchange — W1:** die in a saw lane above a spike gap; the jammed saw becomes your safe bridge.
2. **Spider Umbrella — W1:** intercept a dropping spider high in a chimney; the soul becomes both a kick point and protection from later drops.
3. **Sliding Doorstop — W1:** slide into thorns beneath a descending gate. The flat soul holds a two-wide opening; crawl through on the next life.
4. **Moving Appointment — W2:** launch while charging self-destruct; place a soul where it catches a falling stone and blocks a thrower.
5. **Idol Handoff — W2:** lure the slider onto a plate while jumping, then fence it with a soul. Its weight frees your second soul for a laser shield.
6. **Scarab Delivery — W3:** guide a scarab onto the newest soul above an enclosed plate; recall drops it into its permanent job.
7. **Remove the Floor — W3:** two older souls shield crossing beams; recall your newest foothold to fall safely between their firing lanes.
