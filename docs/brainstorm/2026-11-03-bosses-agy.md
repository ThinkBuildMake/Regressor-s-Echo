## 1. Boss Damage Beyond Stomping
*Why #1 is best:* It turns Recall from a cleanup button into a live trigger you pull when the boss crosses your crosshair, while requiring zero new physics beyond an AABB falling box.

1. **The Suspended Guillotine (World 3 — Stag, developed from seed idea):** Two heavy stone obelisks hang in ceiling chutes at columns 12 and 26. Stepping on a floor switch releases an obelisk; it falls at 400 px/s and stops atop the first soul in its column. To hurt the Stag, catch the obelisk on a high soul during setup, bait the Stag's charge underneath, and tap **Recall** to pull the soul—dropping the stone onto its back to deal 1 hit and shatter the stone. (If a lower soul is older, you must recall in LIFO order or build the catch-soul last!)
2. **Spore-Pod Ricochet / Banking (World 2 — Tree Guardian):** The Tree's lobbed yellow seed pods burst on floors, *but* if a pod hits the vertical side of a mid-air soul, it bounces horizontally toward the Tree. Self-destruct mid-jump at mask height (4 tiles up) so your floating soul acts as a backboard: pod hits soul, ricochets into the stone mask, and cracks it.
3. **Swoop Jam / Collision Stun (World 1 — Moth):** The Moth's dive-swoop passes harmlessly through souls *unless* the soul is a **2-wide slide soul** (denser hitbox). Trick the Moth into firing a shot at you mid-slide at floor level to place a 2-wide soul in its swoop nadir. Next swoop, its horns snag on the wide soul, leaving its head pinned at ground level for 2.5s to stomp or strike.
4. **Root-Tether Counterweight (World 2 — Tree Guardian):** A ceiling boulder hangs over the Tree's mask, counterbalanced by a vine pulley tied to a high left-side platform (row 10). Only **two weight units** on that platform pull the pin. Place Soul 1 on the platform, then stand on it yourself (or place Soul 2 via self-destruct) just as the Tree leans out for its Branch Swing.
5. **Beam Prism / Mirror (World 3 — Stag):** A cracked crystal lens sits in the arena ceiling. If the Stag's blue soul-breaking beam hits a soul placed inside a marked "focus ring" below the lens, the soul shatters *and* refracts a vertical beam down onto the Stag's antlers. Because breaking a soul leaves you short for the Wave, you must survive until a safe window, self-destruct to reload (only during setup) or sacrifice one wall layer and finish the fight on the same cycle.
6. **Sap-Fuse Chain (World 2 — Tree Guardian):** Yellow sap globules drip along the ceiling. A soul placed in a drip lane catches and pools glowing yellow sap (turns volatile after 2s). When the Tree's blue Slam hits a sap-soaked soul, or a second soul links the splash radius to the trunk, it detonates back into the mask.

## 2. World 1 Boss: The Great Moth Spirit (1 Soul, Can't Break Souls)
*Why #1 is best:* It mimics *Hollow Knight*'s Soul Master/Hornet positioning puzzles while making a single soul pull double duty as both vertical shield and horizontal step.

1. **Radial Scale Volley ("Lantern Burst"):**
   - **Tell:** Moth Teleports to top-center (row 4, col 15), folds wings tight, and vibrates with a 0.7s yellow shimmer.
   - **Attack:** Fires 9 yellow dust darts in a 180° downward fan (320 px/s).
   - **Soul Counter:** One standing soul casts a ~3-tile "shadow cone" below and behind it. Stand tight against your soul's flank to stay in the dead zone.
2. **Carpet Duster ("Silken Sweep"):**
   - **Tell:** Snaps to top-left or top-right corner, beating wings rapidly for 0.6s while a vertical column of dust particles forms below it.
   - **Attack:** Flies horizontally across row 4 (280 px/s), dropping a curtain of vertical yellow needles every 2 tiles.
   - **Soul Counter:** A soul placed mid-air (by jumping into an earlier high dart) acts as a permanent umbrella for its 1-tile column—or a 2-wide slide soul on a raised ledge gives a 2-tile safe roof.
3. **Pendulum Swoop ("Crescent Dive"):**
   - **Tell:** Drops to row 11 at the arena edge, rears back for 0.55s with a sharp chirp, scraping the air.
   - **Attack:** Skims the floor across the entire room (500 px/s, 2 tiles tall) before curving back up.
   - **Soul Counter:** Stand on top of a floor soul (1 tile up + jump clears it comfortably), or use the soul as a step to stomp the Moth's back as it passes under you.
4. **Spore Ring ("Halo"):**
   - **Tell:** Pauses wherever it is, curls into a glowing yellow ball for 0.8s.
   - **Attack:** Releases 4 slow-moving dust orbs that orbit outward in a widening square before flying off along the 4 diagonals.
   - **Soul Counter:** Duck behind your soul along the active diagonal so the soul eats the orb headed for your quadrant.
- **Phase Change (After Stomp 2):** The Moth screams and sheds a **permanent downdraft** in the center 6 columns (reduces jump height from 3.4 to 2.2 tiles there), forcing you to place your 1 soul outside the wind tunnel or use a slide-jump across it. Attack gaps drop by ×0.8.

## 3. World 2 (Tree Guardian) & World 3 (Spectral Stag) Attacks
*Why #1 is best:* It turns World 2's self-destruct timer into an active mid-air interception skill rather than just a passive way to drop a block on the floor.

1. **Tree — Canopy Rain + Root Surge (W2 Combo):**
   - **Tell:** Canopy shakes (0.7s yellow glow) as 4 vertical seed-pod lanes light up; simultaneously, the floor brambles glow yellow.
   - **Attack:** Pods rain straight down into the gaps while roots cover the entire floor row for 1.2s.
   - **World 2 Power Answer:** You need one soul on the floor to stand above the roots, and a **second soul self-destructed mid-air** (3 tiles above the first) as an umbrella over that exact column.
2. **Stag — Antler Impale & Toss (W3):**
   - **Tell:** Stag scrapes hoof twice (0.6s blue glow on antlers) and charges only halfway to column 15.
   - **Attack:** Sweeps antlers upward in a 3×3 arc, destroying any soul at ground level and launching two yellow shock-balls backward toward the platform.
   - **World 3 Power Answer:** If you left a ground soul out to bait the Stag, tap **Recall** during its 0.6s tell to snatch the soul back before the antlers shatter it, keeping your soul count intact without healing the boss.
3. **Tree — Creeping Vine Snare (W2):**
   - **Tell:** Mask eyes flash blue (1.5s); a thorny vine burrows along the floor toward your oldest soul.
   - **Attack:** Vine wraps Soul 1 in yellow thorns (making its top lethal to stand on for 6s).
   - **World 2 Power Answer:** Hold **Self-Destruct** to spawn a 3rd soul, intentionally erasing the thorn-wrapped Soul 1 via the 2-soul FIFO cap and shifting your setup one step forward.
4. **Stag — Echo Stampede (W3):**
   - **Tell:** Stag rears up on the right (0.8s yellow flash); two translucent yellow phantom stags form above it at platform height (row 11) and jump height (row 8).
   - **Attack:** Real Stag charges the floor (row 15) while the two phantoms gallop across rows 11 and 8, staggered by 0.35s.
   - **World 3 Power Answer:** Phantoms vanish on hitting a single soul. Stack 2 souls on the platform edge to absorb both phantom lanes, then **Recall** the top soul immediately so you can hop off the edge and stomp the real Stag below.

## 4. The Undodgeable Attack (Critique & Alternatives)
**Critique of the 3-Thick Wall Wave:** Good thematic climax for 3 souls, **but** building 3 adjacent tiles on the same row and standing behind them is spatially static—once built, the player just waits behind a bunker.

*Why #1 is best:* It tests 2D placement, the 3-soul limit, and Recall timing in a single 3-second sequence instead of passive hiding.

1. **Eclipsing Sky + Floor Purge (L-Bunker):**
   - **Telegraph (2.0s):** Arena darkens violet; ceiling runes show vertical arrows (sky laser rain) while floor runes show a horizontal arrow (ground wave, 1 tile tall).
   - **Soul Solution:** Requires a **3-soul L-shape**: Soul 1 on the floor (blocks the horizontal wave), Soul 2 stood on top of Soul 1 (keeps you above the floor purge), and Soul 3 floating 2 tiles above Soul 1 (umbrella against the vertical sky rain).
2. **Sequential Tri-Laser (Recall-Drop Elevator):**
   - **Telegraph (1.8s):** Three full-screen horizontal beams lock at three heights, firing bottom-to-top 0.4s apart; each beam is blocked only along the vertical shadow *under* a soul, or by dropping between safe bands.
   - **Soul Solution:** Build a 3-tall vertical soul stack. Stand on top to clear the bottom sweep, then **tap Recall twice on the beat** (dropping 1 tile per recall) to descend underneath the high sweeps in real time.
3. **Converging Pincer Walls:**
   - **Telegraph (2.2s):** Two violet walls of light spawn at the left and right edges of the arena, sweeping inward at 350 px/s. Each wall breaks 1 soul and stops at the 2nd, or stops if two souls brace each side of the hero.
   - **Soul Solution:** Place 1 soul left, 1 soul right, and stand on the 3rd soul in the middle—catching both walls in a 3-tile safe pocket.

## 5. New Level Mechanics & Room Ideas
*Why #1 is best:* It requires only an AABB overlap check, immediately feels like "cheating" the hazard, and turns every World 2/3 soul into a conductor.

1. **Laser Conduits / Splitter Tiles (W2 & W3):** If a soul is placed inside a glowing "frame tile" in a laser's path, it doesn't just block the beam—it absorbs the energy and shoots a shorter yellow beam 90° upward out of its head. *Trick:* Turn a horizontal murder-beam into a vertical barrier that kills ceiling flyers or triggers a high crystal switch.
2. **Slide-Soul Sled on Crumble Bridges (W1):** A 1-wide standing soul breaks a crumbling tile instantly, *but* a **2-wide slide soul** bridges across two crumble tiles (or spans a 1-tile gap onto a solid ledge), refusing to fall even after the crumble tile underneath vanishes. *Trick:* Slide-die across a fragile bridge to leave a permanent floating plank behind.
3. **See-Saw / Counterweight Beams (W2 & W3):** Two platforms linked by a simple vertical offset (`leftY + rightY = const`). Each soul placed on side A lowers side A by 2 tiles and raises side B by 2 tiles. *Trick (W3):* Stack 2 souls on left, stand on right to reach a high ceiling ledge, then **Recall** both left souls mid-jump to launch the right platform upward for extra boost.
4. **Wind Vents / Updraft Chimneys (W1 & W2):** Vertical columns of rising air push the player upward (and carry hazards). A standing soul caps 1 column of wind; a **2-wide slide soul** caps 2 columns, shutting off the updraft above it so you can drop through spikes safely.
5. **Phase / Ghost Gates (W3):** Violet barrier walls that kill the living hero on contact (so touching one spawns a soul right inside the gate), *or* let you pass while leave-behind souls cannot. *Trick:* Self-destruct as you jump through a gate so your soul clips into the far side to hold a plate, then Recall it through the wall when done.
6. **Magnetic Saw Tracks (W2):** Saws that normally bounce end-to-end along a track. Jamming a saw with Soul 1 stops it; placing Soul 2 and then dying a 3rd time **erases Soul 1**, releasing the saw at the exact moment you need it to slice a vine rope or enemy down-track.
