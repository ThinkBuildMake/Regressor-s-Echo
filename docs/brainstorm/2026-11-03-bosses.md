# Fun pass: bosses, attacks, rooms and projectiles (2026-11-03)

Brainstormed with Codex and agy from the same brief (`2026-11-03-bosses-brief.md`; their full answers sit beside this file), then merged and ranked by Claude. Everything here is **Proposed**: nothing is decided or built yet.

## Rules that came out of the brainstorm
- **One damage method per boss.** Each boss gets one soul trick that opens it up, used three times; no stack of competing weak points. Stomping stays as the way to land the hit once the boss is open.
- **A soul trick opens the boss, not a plain jump.** Every hit needs the soul mechanic: bait, a prop, or a trap you trigger.
- **Never make the player die to recover from an attack.** Any death heals the boss to full, so an attack that destroys souls the player needs forces a heal. Attacks may cost a soul only when there's a way to recover without dying (recall, or a soul that comes back).
- **Attacks run in a fixed loop with recovery gaps, never at random.** Each attack has a tell, and the tells never get shorter; only the gaps between attacks do.

## Powers if recall moves to World 2 (each world keeps the earlier powers)
| World | Souls | Powers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Slide |
| 2 | 2 | Self-destruct, then **recall** (taught a few rooms later: In my own way moves here) |
| 3 | 3 | **Soul swap** (new, recommended): a key swaps places with your newest soul. You teleport to it and it takes your old spot. Not a death, so it never heals a boss |

Soul swap is the recommended World 3 power because it is about one line of position code, it turns every soul into an escape hatch, and the stag is built around it (below). Runners-up: nudge your newest soul one tile; light a soul so it shows hidden platforms.

## Bosses

### The moth (World 1, 1 soul): a Hollow Knight-style loop
Damage: **Lantern Dive into your soul.** The dive stops when it hits a soul (the moth can't break it), the moth is stunned for 2s, and you stomp it. Both Codex and agy ranked this first: one soul is your shield and also the trap.

| Attack | Tell | Hit | Soul answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dust Loom** | Wings shake 0.6s | 3 aimed yellow pods, 0.35s apart, each aimed when fired | Move around your soul so it takes each pod |
| **Lantern Dive** | Wings fold, a yellow lane lights for 0.8s | Dives along the lane (0.5s), recovers 1.2s | Put your soul in the lane: it crashes and is stunned (the damage window) |
| **Pollen Curtain** | Ceiling dots brighten 1s | Dust falls in 3 wide strips, one after another | Shelter under a hanging soul |
| **Ground Skimmer** (from phase 2) | Visibly descends 0.8s | Sweeps the bottom tile | Stand on your soul |

Loop: Loom → Dive → Curtain → a pause to reposition. **After hit 1** its wings tear and Skimmer joins the loop. **After hit 2** Loom fires 4 pods and the recovery gaps shrink ×0.8. Optional (agy): a downdraft in the middle 6 columns in the last phase, so you can't jump as high there.

### The tree guardian (World 2, 2 souls + self-destruct + recall): your stone idea
Damage: **catch a falling stone on a soul, recall the soul to drop it on the mask.** The tree's canopy shake (an attack) drops a stone in a marked column over the mask. Self-destruct mid-air to put a soul under it. The stone sits on that soul until you recall it, which drops it onto the mask. A missed stone breaks on the floor, and a new one comes next canopy shake.

The decoy slam keeps going: blue glow, the soul nearest you is locked, and the slam buries it. If the slam buries your stone soul, the stone falls early and misses. So the two souls have jobs: one holds the stone, the other is the decoy.

| Attack | Tell | Soul answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Canopy shake** | Leaves rain, a stone column lights 1s | Catch the stone on a soul (this is the damage) |
| **Borrowed Fist** (the slam) | Blue arm glow, tether to the locked soul, 2s | Keep the decoy nearer to you than the stone soul |
| **Seed Mortar** | 3 yellow landing circles 0.7s ahead | Lead them away, or let a soul take the last one |
| **Root Rake** | Roots ripple 0.8s, then sweep the floor | Stand on a soul |

Loop: Rake → Mortar → Canopy → Fist. Runner-up damage method (also good): **pod batting**, where a heavy seed bounces off a mid-air soul back into the mask.

### The spectral stag (World 3, 3 souls + soul swap): matador
Damage: **swap out of a charge so it rams your soul.** It charges your position along a yellow lane (0.9s tell). Stand at a soul and swap to another soul at the last moment; the stag rams the soul you left and is dazed 2s under the platform. Drop on it from the platform.

| Attack | Tell | Soul answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Buttress Charge** | Hoof scrape, yellow lane 0.9s | Swap away so it rams a soul: daze, damage |
| **Searching Antlers** (the aimed beam, already built) | Blue aiming line tracks 1.4s, locks 0.4s | Lead it away from souls you need, or swap at the lock |
| **Procession** | 3 yellow floor pulses march toward you, each marked 0.6s ahead | Climb your souls |
| **Last Bell** (the undodgeable attack, below) | Violet countdown rings, 3s | A soul arrangement only |

**Open problem:** if the ram breaks the soul, each hit costs a soul, and replacing it means a death, which heals the stag. **Fix (recommended):** the rammed soul doesn't break, it's knocked flat, so you can swap back onto it next cycle. (Recall still exists in World 3 if it moves to World 2, since powers carry forward, so a stone drop over the charge lane is the runner-up.)

## The undodgeable attack
The current stag wave (breaks 2 souls, stops at the 3rd) has a flaw both reviewers caught: it destroys souls, rebuilding them needs deaths, and deaths heal the boss. So every wave after the first undoes your progress.

1. **Three seals (recommended):** three marked sockets glow on the arena floor and walls. A 3s violet countdown (three shrinking rings). When it ends, a blast fills the arena unless all three sockets hold a soul; the souls survive. It tests where you put your souls, not how fast you rebuild, and it reuses the plate code.
2. **Roof and shutter:** rain from above and a beam from the side at once. One soul over you, one beside you; arrows fill over 2.5s and a bright rectangle previews the safe pocket.
3. **Depth shield:** keep the wall, but each soul absorbs one layer of the wave without breaking (three violet bands peel off as it passes).

## New room mechanics (best first)
1. **Saw Exchange (W1):** die in a saw lane above a spike gap; the jammed saw becomes your bridge over the spikes.
2. **Laser splitter (W2/W3):** a soul inside a marked frame in a laser's path bends the beam 90° upward, to hit a high switch or clear a flyer.
3. **Spider umbrella (W1, with the planned bramble spider):** catch its drop high in a chimney; the soul is both a kick point and a roof against later drops.
4. **Counterweight see-saw (W2/W3):** souls on one side lower it and raise the other; recall mid-jump for a boost.
5. **Slide-soul plank on crumbling bridges (W1):** a lying soul spans two crumbling tiles and stays put after they fall.
6. **Scarab delivery (W3, planned enemy):** walk the scarab out on your souls, then remove the one under it so it drops onto a plate. With recall moved, use soul swap: swap with the soul under it.
7. **Wind vents (W1/W2):** updrafts carry you; a soul caps its column.
